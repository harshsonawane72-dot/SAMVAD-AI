AI-Assisted Support for Every Voice

How It Works

A clear three-step flow for first-contact screening support.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Receive the victim or complainant's interaction through text, chat or voice.

  2. 02

    Analyze

    Analyze language and permitted interaction signals for possible indicators of distress, vulnerability and urgency.

  3. 03

    Assist

    Provide explainable recommendations to trained human personnel for review.

AI assists. Humans decide.

Key Features

Built for responsible decision support during sensitive interactions.

  • Multilingual Ready

    Designed for multilingual victim interactions.

  • Stress Assessment

    Identifies possible conversational indicators of distress.

  • Vulnerability Screening

    Highlights possible vulnerability-related signals.

  • SVI Score

    Generates a transparent prototype Stress Vulnerability Index.

  • Human-in-the-Loop

    Keeps trained personnel responsible for final decisions.

  • Privacy First

    Designed around consent, data minimization and controlled access.

About SAMVAD AI

SAMVAD AI is a prototype of an AI-assisted screening and decision-support layer that can help trained human operators identify possible indicators of stress, vulnerability, and urgency during victim/complainant interactions.

This is a SAMVAD AI Prototype for demonstration purposes. It is not the official NHAA 14566 system.
